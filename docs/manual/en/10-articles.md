# Articles

**Articles** collect knowledge about the builds, for example a FAQ, in one place. Open them with the articles icon in the header or at `/articles`.

## Reading

Everyone can read articles, also without signing in. The list shows the most recently changed articles first. Articles are written in [GitHub-flavored markdown](https://github.github.com/gfm/); raw HTML in an article is shown as text and not run.

## Writing

Users who may write articles see **New article** on the list, and **Edit** and **Delete** on each article. The editor has a **Preview** of the rendered markdown. A title holds up to 200 characters and a body up to 100,000.

Who may write articles is set by an admin under **Settings → General → Article editors**:

| Article editors   | Who may create, edit and delete |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------ |
| Admins            | admins (default)                |
| Leads and admins  | users with the `lead` or `admin` role |
| All users         | every signed-in user with a cisys role |

Any allowed user may edit or delete any article, not only their own.
